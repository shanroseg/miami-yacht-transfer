SMS Terms

Messaging Consent & Terms

Miami Yacht Transfer operates an SMS-based booking service. By texting our number, you are initiating contact and consenting to receive text message replies related to your transfer request.

How opt-in works:
Customers opt in to receive SMS messages by texting our published phone number to request a boat transfer. This inbound text constitutes express consent to receive booking confirmations, status updates, and service-related communications from Miami Yacht Transfer.

Message frequency: You will receive messages only in response to your booking requests — typically 1–3 messages per transfer (confirmation, ETA, and completion).

Opt-out: Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from further messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

Message & data rates may apply depending on your carrier and plan.

We do not share your phone number or personal information with third parties except as necessary to fulfill your transfer (e.g., notifying your captain).
